# Demo script (3 minutes)

**0:00 The problem (20s).** "Registering an OAuth app takes five minutes. Getting it approved so strangers can connect can take two months, and which one you get depends on the exact scopes. Greenlight works out your path."

**0:20 Say what the agent does (30s).** On the landing page type: *agent that triages my users' Gmail and drafts replies*. Point at "Read as: Gmail · Read email · Draft replies · public users". Enter.

**0:50 The verdict (40s).** "5w–2mo, because gmail.readonly is restricted, which means a CASA security assessment." Hover the dots: the verbatim quote from Google's page. Point at the runway: Agent, You, Provider lanes; the orange chain is the critical path.

**1:30 Change your situation (30s).** Tap "Just my team": the runway collapses to today, because internal Workspace apps skip verification (quoted). Tap back. Tap "Up to 100": live today, with the warning about the unverified screen and the lifetime 100-user cap.

**2:00 Faster paths (25s).** Open Faster paths: Composio's approved app today; or a Gmail add-on plus gmail.send, ~6 weeks sooner, with the trade-off. Tap "Use this" and watch the bars move.

**2:25 An app nobody researched (25s).** Type a new app. Five lanes: Scout, Scopes, Gates, Prober, Verifier. Facts tick in; the Verifier strikes one that its quote didn't support. Same guide layout at the end. (If the model is slow, open a finished run from the harness traces.)

**2:50 Close (10s).** "Every number traces to a fact. The harness scores the agents against ten hand-researched providers. Composio could show this at the moment a developer picks managed vs bring-your-own."
